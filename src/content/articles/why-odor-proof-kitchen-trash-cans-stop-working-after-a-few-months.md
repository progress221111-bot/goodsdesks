Odor-proof kitchen trash cans stop working for four distinct reasons: gasket compression set, pedal-hinge geometry drift, plastic micro-porosity, and filter saturation. Each degrades the can in a different way, and two of them get conflated elsewhere even though they aren't the same failure — a lid that no longer seals shut, and liquid that leaks in underneath the liner. Both end with the same symptom, a can that smells again, but they start in different parts of the can and call for different fixes.

Saturation timelines and how these failure modes rank against each other aren't backed by any published data — both depend too much on what a household throws away and how often the can gets cleaned to reduce to a single number.
